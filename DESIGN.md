# Nana Banana Design System

## Overview
Warm, organic, calming visual design system for European nursery wallpaper murals and Etsy listing visuals.
Target audience: Parents shopping on Etsy and Pinterest looking for safe, toxin-free, aesthetically pleasing Montessori & Scandinavian children's interiors.

## Principles
1. **Calm & Tactile (Gemütlichkeit):** No sterile cold blue whites. Backgrounds feel like warm textured linen paper (#FDFBF7).
2. **Clear Readability on Mobile:** Etsy & Pinterest shoppers browse on mobile devices. Clean high-contrast typography, zero clutter.
3. **Organic Softness:** Pill badges, generous padding, soft rounded corners (16px to 24px).

## Component Patterns
- **Badges & USPs:** Pill-shaped capsules (rounded: full) with soft sage or sand tint, dark green text, subtle checkmark icon.
- **Comparison Tables:** Alternating subtle sand stripes (#F3EFEA), rounded outer container with 1px soft border (#E5DFD7).
- **Step-by-Step Guides:** Numbered circle badges with soft sage fill, accompanied by concise action text.

## Do's and Don'ts
- DO use warm charcoal (#2D312E) for text instead of harsh pure #000000.
- DO give elements plenty of breathing room (24px+ whitespace).
- DON'T use saturated neon primaries (no harsh red, electric blue, or harsh yellow).
- DON'T use complex glossy gradients; stick to matte, tactile paper-like aesthetics.
